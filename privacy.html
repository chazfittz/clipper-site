Clipper Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who uses Clipper

Clipper is a private tool used only by its operator to upload short videos to YouTube channels the operator owns. It is not offered to the public.

What data Clipper uses

When the operator connects a YouTube channel, Clipper receives a Google access token for that channel. It uses the token only to upload videos to that channel and to read the channel's basic video statistics through the YouTube Data API.

Clipper does not collect, store or share personal data of any other person. Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are never sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account permissions.

YouTube and Google

Clipper uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
